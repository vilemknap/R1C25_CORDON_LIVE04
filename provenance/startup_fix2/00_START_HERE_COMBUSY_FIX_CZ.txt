RETRY POLITIKA
--------------
Omezeno na prechodna COM volani CALL_REJECTED / RETRY_LATER (HRESULT a
kompatibilni hlasky detekovane puvodnim COM helperem). Bounded per read:
  max_attempts = 64
  initial_delay_seconds = 0.15
  backoff_factor = 1.5
  max_delay_seconds = 1.5
  max_elapsed_seconds = 75
Dalsi presne hodnoty lze volitelne nastavit ve scope:
  com_resilience.scoped_policies.startup_snapshot_read
Kdyz je v existujici konfiguraci com_resilience.enabled = false, nic se
samovolne nezapina. V aktualni Aggressive FIX1 je true.
Vnorenym volanim read_links pod snapshot se NENASOBI casove limity.
